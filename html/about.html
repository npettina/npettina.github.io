About Me

Hi, I'm Noah! I have a hugely diverse background in research mostly spanning the bridge between optics, biophysics, bioinformatics, and most recently astrophysics. I recently (September 2026) started my first year as a Master's student at the University of Padua in Italy studying Astrophysics and Cosmology. While I'm still exploring topics in modern astrophysical research, I'm currently interested in planetary systems: more specifically, I hope to leverage the latest advances in astronomical spectroscopy to study exoplanet atmospheres.

Outside of research, I spend most of my time reading, crafting new recipes in the kitchen, and traveling.
